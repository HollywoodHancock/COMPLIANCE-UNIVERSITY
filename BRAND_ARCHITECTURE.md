# Brand Architecture

## Compliance University™
Parent educational ecosystem.

## NewYorkHUT.com
Educational NY HUT authority site. Owns informational search intent, guides, FAQs, calculators, and regulatory education.

## NYHUT.com
Transactional permit platform. Owns ordering, pricing, payments, customer service, permit processing, and fulfillment.

NewYorkHUT.com content must support qualified referral traffic to NYHUT.com and must not create a competing transactional entity.
