# NewYorkHUT.com → NYHUT.com SEO Policy

## Purpose
NewYorkHUT.com is the educational authority property within Compliance University™. NYHUT.com is the transactional permit-ordering platform.

## Search-intent boundary
- NewYorkHUT.com owns informational and educational intent: requirements, explanations, forms, filing, weight rules, exemptions, comparisons, FAQs, calculators, and regulatory guidance.
- NYHUT.com owns transactional intent: order, buy, apply for service, permit processing, pricing, payment, customer portal, and fulfillment.

## Required behavior
1. NewYorkHUT.com must not contain its own permit checkout or transactional order form.
2. High-intent educational pages must link users to NYHUT.com for ordering.
3. NewYorkHUT.com must not use Product or Offer schema for NY HUT permit services.
4. Content should be original and educational, not duplicated from NYHUT.com commercial landing pages.
5. Canonicals for NewYorkHUT.com content remain self-referencing on NewYorkHUT.com unless a deliberate migration requires otherwise.
6. Compliance University™ may appear as the parent educational brand, while NewYorkHUT.com remains the primary NY HUT educational entity.
7. All SEO expansion must be evaluated for whether it strengthens qualified referral traffic and orders on NYHUT.com rather than competing with it.
