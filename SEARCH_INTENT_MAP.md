Examples: what is NY HUT, who needs NY HUT, weight requirements, filing deadlines, forms, exemptions, OSCAR, IFTA vs HUT, IRP vs HUT, FAQs.

Examples: order NY HUT, buy NY HUT permit, apply for NY HUT service, temporary permit service, pricing, checkout, customer portal.

When an educational page reveals purchase intent, it should direct the user to NYHUT.com rather than create an equivalent commercial page on NewYorkHUT.com.
